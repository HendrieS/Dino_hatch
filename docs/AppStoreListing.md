## English (U.S.)

### Name
Dino Hatch

### Subtitle
Timer that hatches dinosaurs

### Promotional Text
Turn getting ready, tidying up and waiting into a game: when the timer runs out, a dinosaur hatches. Can your child find all 30, including the secret ones?

### Description
Dino Hatch turns any countdown into a dinosaur adventure. Set the timer, and when time is up an egg hatches and a new dinosaur joins your child's collection.

PERFECT FOR EVERYDAY MOMENTS
• Getting ready in the morning
• Brushing teeth and meal time
• Quiet time, focus time and homework
• Waiting your turn or sharing with a sibling
• Moving from one activity to the next

HOW IT WORKS
• Drag around the dial to set a timer of up to 60 minutes
• Watch the egg wobble as time runs down
• When it hatches, meet your new dinosaur and tap See Details to learn all about it
• The timer keeps running even if you leave the app

BUILD YOUR DINO-PEDIA
• 30 illustrated dinosaurs to collect, including secret ones that only appear once you've found all the others
• Kid-friendly facts: when it lived, what it ate, how big it really was and where it was found
• Press and hold to see an X-ray of its skeleton
• Common, Uncommon, Rare and Secret Rare, just like trading cards
• Mark favorites and share a dinosaur card

DINO ALARM
Set a wake-up time for the days you choose. Open the app within 15 minutes of the alarm to hatch a bonus dinosaur and keep your streak going.

WIDGETS AND LOCK SCREEN
• Start a 5, 10, 15 or 30-minute timer right from the Home Screen
• Follow the countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
• See the collection and the next alarm at a glance

MADE FOR KIDS, TRUSTED BY PARENTS
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• We collect nothing: the collection syncs privately through your own iCloud
• Settings sit behind a quick math question for grown-ups
• Optional timer lock, so a running timer can't simply be cancelled
• Unlock fun app icons by hatching dinosaurs

Works on iPhone and iPad, in English, German, Spanish, French, Dutch and Russian.

### Keywords
kids,children,countdown,egg,visual,routine,toddler,preschool,alarm,collection,facts,learning,clock
